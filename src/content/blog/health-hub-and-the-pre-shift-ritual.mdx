Wellness apps built for office workers ask professionals on their feet to meditate at the wrong time and log water like a hobby. Health Hub is narrower and more serious: tools for bodies that are the instrument.

## Daily Pulse

Daily Pulse is a sixty-second check-in — sleep, pain flags, mood, caffeine if you want it. It is not a streak game. Data stays private unless you export it to a provider you choose.

Patterns matter. A trainer noticing hamstring tightness three Mondays in a row can adjust programming before a client bears the cost.

## Cycle Sync

Cycle Sync tracks cycle phases with optional symptom notes for people whose energy and skin change on schedules the booking grid ignores. We do not sell fertility predictions to advertisers. We help you schedule demanding color days when you have bandwidth.

## Med Tracker

Med Tracker reminds you to take prescribed medications and supplements with food requirements that conflict with back-to-back clients. Snooze respects meeting mode. Logs are encrypted at rest.

## Designed for the pre-shift ritual

Health Hub sits in the hour before the work — next to your checklist, not buried in a consumer wellness tab. Large tap targets, readable type, and reduced motion respect settings are default.

## What we will not do

We will not diagnose. We will not replace clinicians. We will not nudge you to buy supplements from a house brand.

We will help you arrive present — which is the minimum professionalism deserves.
