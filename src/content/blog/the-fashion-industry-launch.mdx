On June 30, 2026, fashion industry workflows go live inside Sif's Gold — not as a press stunt, but as connected tools for designers, models, agencies, casting directors, showrooms, brands, and event producers who currently juggle spreadsheets, PDF comp cards, and group chats that were never meant to hold minors' earnings.

## Who it serves

**Designers and brands** manage linesheets, appointments, and showroom calendars without exporting to three other apps.

**Models and agencies** maintain digitized comp cards, measurements, and availability with market-appropriate formats.

**Casting directors** run breakdowns, submissions, and callbacks with audit trails.

**Event producers** coordinate fittings, rehearsals, and call times with the same permission model we use for salon teams.

## Compliance by default

Where laws apply, we encode them as workflows — not footnotes. Fashion Workers Act requirements for covered engagements surface checklists and documentation prompts. Coogan account integration helps agencies and guardians track minors' earnings without improvised spreadsheets.

## Connected to the rest of the platform

Fashion is not a silo. A model who also trains can keep Health Hub data private while sharing bookable availability. A brand working with Gold Partners can commission Advocate-led fit clinics with published revenue splits.

## What launches first

Phase one emphasizes casting, comp cards, showroom scheduling, and compliance packets. Phase two deepens marketplace and production budgets — with the same no-ads, no data-selling rules as the rest of Sif's Gold.

## For members already here

Beauty and grooming members do not need to enable fashion modules. If you touch fashion even occasionally, you can opt in when the work appears.

We are late to fashion only if you measure by press releases. Measured by lawful, usable tools, we are on time with the people who asked us to build.
