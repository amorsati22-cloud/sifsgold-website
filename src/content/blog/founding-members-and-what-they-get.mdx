Founding Members are the people who help us harden Sif's Gold while the paint is still wet — schools running cohorts, shops migrating chairs, Advocates publishing first paths, fashion teams testing casting workflows.

They are not a VIP club for hype. They are a time-boxed partnership with obligations on both sides.

## What Founding Members receive

Founding Members get locked pricing for the founding window, early access to features before public marketing, direct office hours with product, and recognition inside The Gold Collective where they want it.

They also get honesty when something is not ready. Early access is not early chaos without support.

## The window

The founding window closes on a published date — not a fake countdown. When it closes, new members move to standard tiers. Founding pricing honors renewals according to the terms shown at signup.

## Who is joining

We are seeing independent studios, student cohorts from partner schools, barbershops that never had software they trusted, and fashion agencies that need Coogan-compliant workflows alongside casting boards.

## What we ask in return

Founding Members file clear bug reports, attend occasional feedback calls, and accept that labels like "beta" mean something. We ask for patience with rough edges and refusal to tolerate harm or bias.

## After the window

Founding Member status does not expire, but perks tied to the window do. We will always mark who helped us launch — because launch is a verb sustained by real shops, not a press release.

If you are considering joining, read the terms, ask hard questions on office hours, and join only if the trade makes sense for your business.
