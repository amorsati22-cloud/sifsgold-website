If you subscribe inside a mobile app, Apple and Google take up to thirty percent of recurring revenue in many categories. That slice does not come from our margin alone — it comes from education payouts, Advocate shares, and the roadmap.

We route subscriptions through the web on purpose.

## What stays in the app

Small, one-time purchases that belong beside the work — a board cram pack, a template, a single live ticket — can stay in-app where the fee is proportional to the value.

## What moves to the web

Memberships, school licenses, and Advocate revenue-bearing plans checkout on the web with transparent pricing pages you can screenshot for your accountant. You sign in to the same account afterward.

## Why this helps creators and pros

When less revenue vanishes to platform rent, more can flow to Sif's Advocates and Gold Partners under published splits. Schools can budget honestly. Solo pros know the number on the pricing page is the number that funds features.

<PullQuote author="Magnus Holm">
  I would rather tap a link once than wonder why my favorite educator's course price jumped to feed a store tax.
</PullQuote>

## Security and receipts

Web checkout uses the same payment processors we document in our transparency pages. Receipts email immediately. Cancellations follow published refund policy — no maze of app store dispute screens.

## Plain talk

We are not anti-app. We are anti-surprise. The web path is how we keep more of your money in the industry that earned it.
