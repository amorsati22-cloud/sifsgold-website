An ad-supported product is never really free. The price is attention, inference, and the slow drift of product decisions toward what maximizes impressions instead of what helps a nail tech finish a day on time.

We chose a different economics model from day one.

## What ads cost, even when you do not click

When a platform monetizes through advertising, every screen becomes a contest for dwell time. Notifications get louder. Feeds get noisier. Sensitive professional data becomes more valuable as targeting signal. You are not the customer — the advertiser is.

We are not willing to run that experiment on license numbers, client health notes, or a student's board prep history.

## How we fund the work instead

Member subscriptions, Founding Member windows, and fair shares with Sif's Advocates and Gold Partners pay for engineering, compliance, and support. Micro-purchases that belong in the app — a one-off template, a premium study pack — stay in-app. Recurring subscriptions route through the web so more revenue stays with the people who earned it, not with app store rent.

<PullQuote author="Magnus Holm">
  I have paid for three "free" apps with my client list showing up in remarketing. Once was enough.
</PullQuote>

## Privacy as a product decision

Privacy policy language only matters if the business model allows it. Ad-free is not a footnote for us; it is the reason we can commit to no data selling, clear export paths, and industry-aligned defaults for med spa and fashion workflows.

We will publish what we collect, why we collect it, and how to delete it — in plain language, updated when behavior changes.

## What this means for you

You can open Sif's Gold to study, book, or cast without wondering whether your search history becomes someone else's campaign. Your work stays yours. Our incentive is to keep you successful enough to stay — not to keep you scrolling.

That is the deal. We intend to keep it.
