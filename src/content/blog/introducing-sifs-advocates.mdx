Sif's Advocates are educators, artists, and practitioners who teach on Sif's Gold — not influencers rented for a weekend campaign. We built their program around five revenue streams because a single "views" number has never paid a colorist's rent.

## Why view-based payments fail

View counts reward platforms, not teachers. A fifteen-minute balayage breakdown can change someone's career and barely register in an algorithm tuned for outrage. We do not pay Advocates based on vanity metrics.

Instead, revenue ties to value delivered: enrollments, referrals, digital goods, live sessions, and partnership work with Gold Partners inside The Gold Collective.

## The five streams

**Courses and paths.** Structured lessons with clear outcomes — board prep supplements, business labs, advanced technique series.

**Digital goods.** Templates, formulas, and toolkits members purchase outright.

**Live sessions.** Office hours, critiques, and Q&A with transparent scheduling and payouts.

**Referrals.** When an Advocate helps a school or studio adopt Sif's Gold, compensation is contractual and reported.

**Gold Partner collaborations.** Brands and schools fund co-created content with Advocates, split according to published terms — not hidden in a "creator fund" blog post.

## Tools Advocates actually use

Advocates get dashboards for enrollment, refunds, and tax reporting exports — boring on purpose. Payouts are monthly with line items, not a single opaque pool.

We also give Advocates the same accessibility and language standards we hold the rest of the product to. Captions, transcripts, and readable slides are required, not optional extras.

## Joining the program

Applications open with clear eligibility: verified license or documented expertise, sample lesson, and agreement to community guidelines. We review for quality, not follower count.

If you teach for a living — not for a logo placement — Sif's Advocates was built for you.
