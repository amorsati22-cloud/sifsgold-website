We did not set out to build another app with a salon module taped on the side. We set out to build the place where a student in Boise, a barber in Brooklyn, a trainer in Austin, and a casting assistant in Los Angeles can do real work without switching tabs, without selling their attention, and without wondering who owns their client list tomorrow.

The beauty, grooming, fitness, and fashion industries share more than people admit. The same person who books color appointments also tracks continuing education hours, manages a chair rental, and follows casting calls for a side project. Tools treated these lives as separate products. We did not.

## What we believe

We believe your data is yours. We will not run display advertising on your work. We will not sell behavioral profiles to data brokers. Subscriptions and fair revenue shares with Sif's Advocates and Gold Partners fund the platform — not your inbox.

We believe gender parity is a design requirement, not a marketing line. Barbershops are not an afterthought. Men's grooming is not a skin on a women's salon product. Profile themes, default language, and content libraries are audited for balance before features ship.

We believe accessibility is part of craft. WCAG 2.1 AA is our baseline for reading, navigation, and motion. If a student cannot study for boards with a screen reader, we have missed the point.

## What is live at launch

At launch you will find state board prep for all fifty states — roughly three hundred items per jurisdiction with statute citations, vendor references, and passing scores where states publish them. Licensed professionals get booking, client records, and education tracking built for chair economics, not enterprise salon chains. Students get structured study paths. Sif's Advocates get transparent revenue tools that do not depend on vanity view counts.

Health Hub ships with Daily Pulse, Cycle Sync, and Med Tracker because the hour before the work is not optional for people on their feet ten hours a day.

## What is coming

Fashion industry workflows land June 30, 2026 — designers, models, agencies, casting directors, showrooms, brands, and event producers on the same rails as the chair. Founding Members receive a defined window of perks while they help us harden workflows in the field.

<PullQuote author="Astrid Linnea">
  We stopped duct-taping five apps together the week our school adopted one study path per state. That alone changed our pass rates.
</PullQuote>

We are building in public through this journal. You will read honest notes on pricing, privacy, and the tradeoffs we make — including why subscriptions route through the web and why we will never run ads.

If you work in these industries, you already know the cost of fragmentation. We are here to pay down that cost with you — not with hype, but with software that respects the work.

Thank you for reading. Welcome to Sif's Gold.
