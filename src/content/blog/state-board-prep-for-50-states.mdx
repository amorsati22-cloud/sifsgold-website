Generic cosmetology question banks fail students for a simple reason: boards are state law exams dressed up as beauty tests. A sanitation rule in Florida is not the sanitation rule in Minnesota. Pretending otherwise wastes study hours and erodes trust.

We mapped all fifty states.

## Depth per jurisdiction

Each state library targets roughly three hundred items — not three hundred repeats of national trivia. Items reference state statutes where boards publish them, approved vendor lists when states maintain them, and passing scores when those numbers are public.

Students see why an answer is correct, not just that it is.

## Why "close enough" hurts

Schools lose accreditation pressure when pass rates slip. Students lose fees and weeks when they sit for the wrong emphasis. Magnus Holm, an educator we interviewed in Saint Paul, put it plainly: "My class does not need another generic app. They need Minnesota."

## How we maintain content

Regulatory changes trigger review queues — not annual PDF dumps. When a state board updates its candidate handbook, our content team patches items, logs the revision date, and notifies enrolled students.

We also separate practice modes: untimed study, timed exam simulation, and weak-area drills based on missed tags.

## For schools

School administrators get cohort reporting — not to surveil, but to spot chapters where an entire class struggles. That is a curriculum signal, not a grading weapon.

## What students should expect

If you are studying with Sif's Gold, you will still need your school's guidance and the official candidate bulletin for your state. We make the path legible. You still walk it.

Board prep is not glamorous work. It is the work that lets the glamorous work happen legally. We respect that.
